You are given a flat rock by a lake and one stone at a time. Drag the stone to
where you want it, let go, and it settles onto the cairn. Then the next one.

The twist is where the ground is. The rock is your phone, so the real direction
of gravity decides which way the stones want to slide. Lean the phone a little
and the cairn leans with it; tilt it back the other way and you can catch a
stack that has started to sway. Even the small movements of your hand reach the
stones, which is why it pays to breathe out and keep still while you build.
Lay the phone flat and there is no down left at all, and the cairn comes apart.

No two stones are the same. Each one is shaped and coloured on the spot, from
light granite and warm sandstone to dark basalt, some with a white quartz vein
running through them. Flat ones are easy to build on, rounded ones rock, and
part of the game is choosing where each one goes and how to turn it.

Calm is the game at its simplest: build until the cairn falls. Challenge adds
gusts of wind that grow stronger the higher you build, a shakier hand, stronger
tilt and rounder stones, with a record of its own.

Everything happens on the phone and there is no account. Your best heights are
saved on the device.
