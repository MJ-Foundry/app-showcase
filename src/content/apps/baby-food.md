Starting solids produces exactly the kind of information you think you'll
remember and then don't: which foods have been tried, which one preceded the
rash, and whether egg has been offered three times or once.

Each entry is a food, a date and a reaction, which is enough to answer those
questions later. The allergen view tracks the major allergens specifically —
what's been introduced, how many times, and what reaction followed — and
suggests what to try next.

On-device only, with multiple child profiles and a JSON export for backups.
