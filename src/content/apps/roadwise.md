Passing the written test is the easy part — remembering what two hundred
similar-looking triangles and circles mean six months later is the part that
actually matters on the road. Roadwise turns that memorization into a quick
quiz instead of a flashcard chore.

Questions are weighted rather than random: signs you have not seen yet or
keep getting wrong show up far more often than ones you have already
mastered, with a small boost for anything you have not been quizzed on in a
while. A daily streak gives the repetition some pull.

Everything — signs, images and your progress — ships with the app and stays
on the device. There is no account and no sync. The one thing that can leave
the device is anonymous usage statistics, and they stay off until you turn
them on.
