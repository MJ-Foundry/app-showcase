Good light is most of a good photo, and the brightest, most color-accurate panel
most people own is already in their pocket. Softbox turns a phone into a
controllable fill light: one flat surface of color, as bright as the display can
go.

A single tap brings up the controls — intensity and your saved favorites. A
double tap opens the color picker. Dual-color mode splits the screen into two
tones for a warmer key and a cooler rim, which is more useful than it sounds
when you're lighting a face.

Everything runs on the device with no account and no network.
