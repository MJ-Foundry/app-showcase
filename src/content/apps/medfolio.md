Running out of a prescription is a scheduling failure, not a memory failure. The
information needed to prevent it is already known: how many pills are in the box,
how many you take a day, and when you last restocked.

Medfolio does that arithmetic continuously and shows the only number that
matters — days left — then fires a local reminder while there's still time to get
to the pharmacy. Scanning a package barcode is the fast path: scan once and the
app remembers which medication that code belongs to, so future restocks are a
single tap.

Swedish interface, on-device storage, no account.
