The receipt you need is always the one you have thrown away. Receipt Keeper
keeps them instead: snap a photo of a receipt or a warranty card and the app
recognizes the text right on the device, guessing the store, the date and the
amount so you rarely have to type anything.

Once a receipt is in, it is searchable — by shop or by the products on it — so
returning a faulty toaster or finding proof of purchase for a claim is a matter
of a quick search rather than a rummage through a drawer. Return windows and
warranty periods become dated reminders, and the app nudges you before either
one closes.

Everything happens locally. The text recognition runs on the device, nothing
is uploaded, and there is no account to create — your receipts stay yours.
