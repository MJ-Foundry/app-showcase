Knots is a small, calm teacher for the knots people actually use. Pick one
and the rope grows into it a step at a time: the standing part first, then
the working end finding its way over, under and through. Each step has a
single short line, like "up through the loop", and nothing else to read.

The knot is real 3D, so you can turn it round with a finger and look at it
from the side or from behind, which is where most knot diagrams leave you
guessing. Double-tap to put it back.

The part that makes it stick is tying it yourself. The working end of the rope is
light, and a faint guide shows where it has to go. Drag the end along it,
over and under, until the knot is done. Knots you have tied by hand get a
tick in the list.

There are 114 of them, from the overhand and the bowline to lashings,
binding knots and decorative ones, grouped the way you would look for them:
stoppers, loops, bends for joining two ropes, hitches for tying on to
things, and so on. Search works in Swedish and English, and the activity
chips narrow the list to what you need for the boat, the crag or the tent.

Everything stays on the device. There is no account, no sync, no analytics
and no ads.
