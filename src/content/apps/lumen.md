Lumen is a writing environment built around **focus**. The interface fades away
while you draft, then surfaces gentle, contextual assistance exactly when you
ask for it — never before.

Under the hood, Lumen keeps your document as portable Markdown and runs an
optional on-device model so sensitive drafts never leave your machine. When you
do want the cloud, it streams suggestions with sub-100ms latency.

It's the tool I reach for whenever I need to think clearly and write well.
