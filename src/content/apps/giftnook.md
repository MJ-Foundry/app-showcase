Giftnook is a warm, calm home for everything gift-related — not a spreadsheet.
It keeps track of the ideas you have, the gifts you've bought, and the little
details worth remembering about the people in your life, so birthdays and
Christmas stop catching you out.

Each person gets a light profile — their interests and favourites, sizes and
colours, plus a history of what you've given before. Group gifts under an
occasion like *Christmas 2026* or a birthday, and a gentle countdown keeps it on
your radar. Jot an idea, then move it from idea to bought to given with a single
tap, adding a price, a link and a store along the way.

When it's time to shop, the "To buy" list gathers everything you haven't given
yet and groups it by store — so you can order from one shop all at once. A quiet
running budget keeps the total in view without getting in the way. Everything is
stored on your phone: no account, no backend.
