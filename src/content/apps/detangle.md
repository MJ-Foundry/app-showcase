A handful of rope lies in a wooden tray, crossed and woven the way rope ends
up when it has been dropped in a heap. Some of it lies on top, some of it is
caught underneath, and here and there a rope has tied itself into a knot.

Pull a rope and it moves like rope: the rest of it follows, it drags against
the wood and against the ropes lying on it, and it settles where you leave it.
A rope that has nothing lying on top of it and no knot in it is free, and it
lifts out of the tray by itself. Sometimes that frees the next one too.

The tray is your phone. Tilt it and the ropes slide toward the low side, give
it a small shake and the pile loosens. Knots come undone the way they do in
your hands, by opening the loop and drawing the end back through.

The game works offline, needs no account and collects nothing. Your progress
is saved on the device.
