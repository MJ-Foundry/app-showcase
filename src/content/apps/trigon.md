Two players take turns drawing lines between six dots. The moment three of your
own lines close a triangle, you lose — and a triangle counts even where two of
your lines simply cross, not only where they meet at a dot. That is what makes
it tricky: a line that looks harmless can finish a triangle somewhere out in the
middle of the board.

It is based on the old pen-and-paper game Sim, with that crossing rule added on
top — which turns a handful of traps into more than a hundred. A tie is not
possible, so every game ends with someone caught out.

Play by passing one phone back and forth, or against the computer at three
levels. The hardest one looks ahead to the end of every game, so it never makes
a mistake. Everything runs offline, and the only thing saved is your settings
and your record against the computer.
