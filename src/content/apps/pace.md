Most habit apps treat a missed day as a failure. **Pace** treats it as data.

The app is built around forgiving streaks that flex with your life, glanceable
widgets, and a calm design language that rewards consistency without shaming
lapses. It's fully offline and stores nothing on a server — your routines are
nobody's business but yours.
