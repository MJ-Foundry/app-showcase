Prism generates colour systems that are both **beautiful and usable**. Every
scale is built in OKLCH for perceptual evenness, and every pairing is scored
against WCAG contrast thresholds in real time.

Export straight to CSS custom properties, a Tailwind theme, or Figma tokens — no
copy-pasting hex codes by hand.
