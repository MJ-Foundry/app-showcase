Roadwise Privacy Policy

Last updated August 14, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy covers Roadwise, the road-sign quiz app published by MJ Foundry. Our other apps are covered by our general policy, because they behave differently — Roadwise is the only one that can send anything at all, and it does so only if you ask it to.

The signs, the images and your progress all ship inside the app and stay on your device. There is no account, no sign-up and no sync. The one thing that can leave your device is anonymous usage statistics, and they are switched off until you turn them on.

What We Collect

Nothing, unless you opt in.

The first time you open Roadwise we ask, once, whether you are willing to share anonymous usage statistics. Until you answer yes, nothing is collected and nothing is transmitted. If you decline, or simply ignore the question, that remains true forever.

If you do turn it on, we collect app events: which screens you open, when a quiz is started and finished, how many answers were correct, and which sign categories you practise. Alongside those, Firebase attaches standard technical details — app version, device model, operating system version, and the country your connection appears to come from.

What We Never Collect

Your name, your email address, your phone number, your precise location, your advertising ID, and your device’s Android ID. None of these are collected, at any point, whatever you choose.

Anything you type into the app — including the search box in Bläddra — is never sent anywhere. It is used on your device to filter the list and then discarded.

We have switched off Google Signals, advertising-ID collection and ad-personalisation signals in our Firebase project, and the app is built with the advertising-ID permission removed entirely. The data cannot be used to build an advertising profile or be joined to your Google account.

The Anonymous Identifier

If you opt in, the app generates a random identifier on your device — a UUID, not derived from anything about you or your hardware. It is the only identifier attached to the statistics, alongside the app-instance ID that Firebase creates for the same installation.

Neither is shared with any other app. Neither survives a reinstall. We have no way to connect either one to a person, which is the point.

You can see your identifier in the app under Framsteg, below the settings. It is shown there so that you can quote it to us if you ever want your data deleted — without it we genuinely cannot tell your records apart from anyone else’s.

Turning Statistics On or Off

You are asked once, on first run, and your answer is remembered. You can change it at any time under Framsteg → Inställningar, with the switch labelled “Anonym statistik”. Turning it off stops collection immediately, clears the locally stored events that have not been sent, and discards the identifier.

Turning it off does not by itself erase what was already collected. That data expires on its own within two months, and you can have it deleted sooner by emailing us the identifier described above.

Uninstalling the app removes the identifier and everything else stored locally.

How Long We Keep It

Two months. Firebase is configured to delete event-level data automatically after that period, which is the shortest retention Google Analytics offers, and we only ever look at aggregate reports.

How We Use It

To make the app better — to see which parts people actually reach, which sign categories are hardest, and where the quiz is too slow or simply ignored.

We do not sell, rent or trade it. We do not use it for advertising. We do not share it with anyone other than Google in its role as our processor.

Your Rights

If you are in the EU, the EEA or the UK, MJ Foundry is the data controller for the usage statistics described here, and Google acts as our processor.

Our legal basis is your consent — Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR. You give it by accepting the question on first run, and you can withdraw it at any time, without giving a reason, using the switch in the app. Withdrawing is as easy as giving it, and it does not make anything you did beforehand unlawful.

You have the right to ask what data we hold about you, to receive a copy of it, to have it corrected or deleted, to have its processing restricted, and to object to it. Because none of it is tied to your identity, we need the anonymous identifier from the app in order to find your records — paste it into your email and that is enough for us to act.

Email mjfoundry.support@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to your national data protection authority; in Sweden that is Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY), imy.se.

Where the Data Goes

Usage statistics are processed by Google and may be stored on, or accessed from, servers in the United States. We rely on Google’s standard data processing terms, the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, and Google’s certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework as the legal basis for that transfer.

If you never turn statistics on, nothing about you leaves your device and no transfer takes place.

Third-Party Services

Google Analytics for Firebase, described above, and nothing else. No advertising, no crash reporting, no attribution, no tracking SDKs of any kind.

If you download the app from Google Play, the store may independently collect information under its own privacy policy — for example download counts or aggregate crash statistics. That collection is governed by the store’s policy, not ours, and we only ever see the aggregate figures the store makes available to any developer.

Children’s Privacy

Roadwise is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age. The statistics described here are anonymous and only collected after someone actively turns them on.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, email mjfoundry.support@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We update this policy before a change ships, not after. If Roadwise starts or stops collecting something, this page changes in the same release and the date at the top changes with it.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, or want to exercise any of the rights described above, email us at mjfoundry.support@gmail.com.
